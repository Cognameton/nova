WHY
---
The research log's diagnosis of the three-era collapse (F12/F13, O3): a
closed loop with no exogenous input — a fixed point, not a capability
ceiling. Every tool on the tick surface returns Nova's own text
(recall_self, reflect, heartbeats, explorations, findings) or the
operator's disposition of it (recall_history 'outcomes'). The 22.11 draft
deferred "an external text source". A game is smaller and stricter than a
text source: the board is the same for anyone who looks at it, a move is
legal or it is not, and the score is arithmetic. It is the first thing in
her context she did not write.

Operator framing (2026-09-18): "maybe we need to give her something to
do." No mandate is placed on the surface — take-up is itself the first
measurement.

WHAT
----
src/nova/agent/reversi.py (new)
  Rules: 8x8, standard opening (d4/e5 = O, e4/d5 = X). Nova is X and
  moves first. Pure functions: new_board, legal_moves, flips_for,
  apply_move, score, render_board ('*' marks her legal squares).
  Opponent: 'greedy' (most flips, seeded tie-break) or 'random'. The
  RNG is seeded from (game seed, move count) so a game replays exactly
  from its record.
  ReversiStore: data_dir/games/reversi_current.json (one active game),
  reversi_games.jsonl (finished games, full move list, comments,
  tick_ref per Nova move, illegal_attempts, final_score, result).
  ReversiController.play(move, comment): 'new' starts; a square with
  no game open starts and plays; 'resign' ends as a loss; illegal or
  malformed squares are counted, not applied, and the board is
  unchanged. The opponent replies inside the same call; forced passes
  are resolved by the runtime — whenever she sees the board, she has a
  legal move. Game end -> finished record, current cleared.
  prompt_block(): the [Reversi] block (record, score, board, legal
  moves, last two moves), or "No game in progress" with the record.
  render_play_result(): compact carryover text.

Wiring
  config.py: GameConfig {reversi_enabled=False, reversi_opponent=
    'greedy', reversi_seed=None}; validated.
  tool_registry.py: play_reversi ToolSpec (allowed, non-destructive,
    internal).
  self_state_tools.py: 'play_reversi' in SELF_STATE_TOOL_NAMES (always
    parseable; dispatch answers reversi_unavailable when disabled);
    dispatcher takes reversi_controller; CARRYOVER_TOOL_NAMES =
    READ_TOOL_NAMES | {play_reversi}; render_read_tool_result handles it.
  self_state_tick.py: '{game_tool}' slot in _SYSTEM; menu suffix and
    tool sentence in BOTH registers when enabled; [Reversi] block in
    the user context after the tool-results block. When disabled the
    rendered prompt is byte-identical to 22.12 (tested).
  runtime.py: controller built in __init__ from config.game; block
    passed on every tick; controller passed to the dispatcher;
    carryover uses CARRYOVER_TOOL_NAMES.

Tests: tests/test_reversi.py (24) — rules, playouts terminate, seed
replay, store persistence, illegal handling, prompt surfaces,
byte-identity when off, end-to-end daemon tick (board in prompt,
