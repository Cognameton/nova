MISSION
-------

Run Nova live: the daemon persistent between sessions, real
conversations, register-aware baseline collection from day one. Phase
21 proved the instrument on a single afternoon; Phase 22 accumulates
the longitudinal record the instrument exists to collect — steady-state
exploration rates (was 61% appetite, novelty, or attractor loop?),
cross-session pattern persistence (the ladder's L1/L2 bars become
satisfiable for the first time), and the first operator promotions
with live perturbation probes.

Phase 22 is NOT: fine-tuning (Phase 23+, bound by the operator's
recorded corpus-policy acknowledgment), new action surfaces, TTS/STT,
or any relaxation of operational boundaries.
