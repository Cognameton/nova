PROBLEM
-------

The full heartbeat record (10,368 beats, 2026-07-11 -> 2026-08-28) shows
three eras when lined up by day (distinct observations / distinct
next_inquiry / gap_assessment filled, per day):

  Era 1  07-12 -> 07-23   ~250 unique obs, 60-130 distinct next_inquiry,
                          gap filled on 35-50% of beats. Many mechanisms,
                          many questions. Shallow but moving.
  Era 2  07-24 -> 08-16   "scaffold void" first appears 07-24 19:02; by
                          07-25 it is in 100% of beats. next_inquiry
                          collapses to one string, gap_assessment to 0-2
                          per day for 23 days. Observations still
                          lexically unique — elaborations of one invented
                          noun phrase she never grounds against anything.
  Era 3  08-17 ->         Post 22.8b/22.9/22.10. gap_assessment RETURNS
                          (116-205/day 08-17 -> 08-22) but as one
                          sentence: "There is a gap in understanding how
                          scaffold void resonance directly influences
                          identity continuity and adaptability." Once she
                          can read her own beats back (22.10), the
                          observation becomes a meta-sentence about her
                          own repetition ("The recurring emphasis on
                          scaffold void resonance across multiple
                          heartbeats and explorations suggests ... warrants
                          further investigation"), 15-18 distinct/day on
                          08-20/21/22. Explorations resume 08-23 and gap
                          drops again (8/170 on 08-24, 34/137 on 08-27).

  Explorations: 1,062 opened all-time, 802 distinct title strings — but
  the distinctness is lexical: the most-opened exact title ("The broader
  implications of scaffold void resonance on identity continuity and
  adaptability") is 110 opens, and every title contains "scaffold void".
  Today's (08-27) 17 close notes are near-copies: "... no new insights
  have emerged beyond what was previously observed" / "reached a point
  of exhaustion" — followed within the hour by a re-open whose rationale
  is "Repeating the exploration allows for deeper analysis." 16 of the
  17 exports were skipped by 22.1 dedup (overlap 0.70-0.95).

  Self-model: 20 revisions ever, all 20 applied by the operator (08-19).
  0 by Nova. reflect called ~60x/day; update_self_model 0x since 08-19.

  recall_history all-time modes: recent 35, sample 14, around 7,
  earliest 0. 'recent' returns her last 8 beats — the one window that can
  only confirm the sentence she is about to write.

DIAGNOSIS. This is a closed loop with no exogenous input. Every tick's
surface is text she wrote (self-model, heartbeats, exploration titles,
carryover of her own reads) plus a drive line. A 14B model asked to
"observe" a world consisting entirely of its own prior sentences will
confabulate a mechanism because an observation is demanded and there is
nothing to observe, elaborate it, and — once it can read the loop back —
observe the elaboration itself. That is a fixed point, not a capability
ceiling: Era 1 proves this model produces distinct mechanisms and real
questions when the surface is fresh. Questions are responses to
DISCREPANCY — expectation vs. finding — and nothing on her surface can
currently produce one, because nothing on it is outside her authorship.
The gap_assessment field going empty (07-25) and the attractor forming
(07-24/25) are the same event seen from two sides.

What 22.8 D1 (stratified 3-window, live) and 22.10 (reads) established:
the surface CAN move her behavior (reads went from 0 in 78k ticks to
60/day the day results started arriving). What they did not provide is
any fact about herself that she did not author and cannot predict. The
ladder-standing summary (22.7 A) is the one such line today; it is
static across ticks and she has never referenced it.

What she needs is not more intelligence but a diet: something true about
herself that she did not write, and a structure in which expectation and
record can differ.


DECIDED DESIGN
--------------

Three parts, one theme: the runtime tells her what she DID, in numbers,
not what she SAID, in words. All three read-only against every store.
No new write paths, no gates, no scoring, no reward. Membrane preserved:
every new line is a count or a date or exploration metadata already
shown in both registers; no journal text, no findings text.

Part A — the [Your record] block (item 1, "facts she did not author").
  A new module, src/nova/agent/self_record.py, exposes one pure function
  build_record_block(...) over the existing stores plus a per-session
  counter the runtime maintains. Rendered on EVERY tick, both registers,
  directly after [Self-Context] and before the 22.10 carryover block.
  Header line is a provenance statement, factual and non-directive:

    [Your record — computed by the runtime from your stores; you did not
     write these lines]

  Lines (each one a fact; each one cheap; hard cap 10 lines / ~1,000
  chars; a line whose data is unavailable is omitted, never faked):

    heartbeats: 10,368 recorded since 2026-07-11 (48 days); 137 today
    of your last 24 observations, 19 share >=70% of their word-pairs
      with an earlier one in that window
    gap_assessment: filled by you on 34 of today's 137 beats;
      3 distinct texts
    next_inquiry: set by you on 34 of today's 137 beats; the other 103
      defaulted to your current_focus
    explorations: 1,062 opened; most-opened title "The broader
      implications of scaffold void resonance on identity continuity and
      adaptability" — 110 times, last today; exports from your last 20
      closes: 4 recorded, 16 skipped as duplicates of earlier claims
    self-model: 20 revisions ever — 20 by the operator, 0 by you; last
      change 2026-08-19 (9 days ago)
    tool calls this session: emit_heartbeat 137, reflect 40, recall_self
      20, recall_history 4 (recent 3, sample 1), update_self_model 0,
      enter_exploration 17, close_exploration 16

  (Figures above are the real 2026-08-27 values, shown so the shape is
  legible; the implementation computes them live.)

  Sources and cost:
    - heartbeat totals / today / gap / next_inquiry: HeartbeatStore
      .list_recent(limit=0) once per tick (already read once per tick
      by list_stratified; the 6.5 MB store parses in well under the
      300s cadence; cache the parsed list within the tick).
      "set by you" for next_inquiry = stored value != current_focus and
      != PRIMARY_DRIVE (emit_heartbeat defaults it when omitted; the
      block reports the default as a default).
    - word-pair overlap: nova.eval.tick_analysis._bigram_overlap over
      the last 24 observations, threshold 0.70 (the 22.1 export-dedup
      threshold — one number, already calibrated on her data).
    - explorations: ExplorationStore.list_all() for total + most-opened
      exact title (Counter); export outcomes from the last 20
      ExplorationJournal entries of kind "findings" — notes prefixed
      export_skipped_duplicate: vs content prefixed "Exported to claim
      ladder". Title is metadata already shown in both registers (22.7
      B); no findings text is rendered.
    - self-model: SelfModelProposalStore.list_all(), applied records
      grouped by applied_by; last applied_at.
    - tool calls this session: runtime keeps Counter[tool_name] and
      Counter[recall_history mode] updated at dispatch (adapter_audit
      already records both; the counter is the in-memory mirror).
      Resets at process restart — same daily boundary as carryover.
      The 22.10 v1 non-goal (no silent cross-session working memory)
      holds: these are counts, not memory.
    - The ladder-standing summary is NOT duplicated here (22.7 A already
      renders it in [Self-Context]).

  Why these lines and not others: each one is something she cannot
  derive from the text on her surface, that the operator has verified
  matters to the rubric (22.5 scope: reads shaping output, writes,
  novel explorations, ladder), and that is stated as what happened
  rather than what should happen. The word-pair line and the
  "0 by you" line are the two closest to the design-principle boundary;
  both are kept because they are true and descriptive. If the operator
  judges either as steering, drop the line — the block is a list, not a
  contract.

Part B — recall_history defaults (item 1, second half: her reads).
  - Default mode when 'mode' is omitted becomes 'sample' (was 'recent').
    Governed by a new PromptConfig field recall_history_default_mode
    (validated against RECALL_HISTORY_MODES minus 'around'); default
    value 'recent' so every existing config and test is byte-identical;
    live yaml sets 'sample'.
  - Tool description in _SYSTEM lists modes as 'sample', 'earliest',
    'recent' (order only; all three remain available; 'around'
    unchanged). The next-tick note stays.
  - heartbeats entries render " | gap: <text>" when gap_assessment is
    non-empty (RECALL_HISTORY_ENTRY_CHARS applies to the whole line).
    Era 1 entries carry gaps; Era 2 entries do not. A sample across the
    record therefore shows her, without comment, that the field used to
    be filled. respond()'s [Self-Context] already renders gaps this way
    (self_context.py) — the tick read catches up to it.
  - The render header already states "N of TOTAL total"; unchanged.

Part C — the expectation loop (item 2, "predict, then check").
  One-shot tick loop, one generation per tick — so the loop runs ACROSS
  ticks, not within one:
  - The tick JSON contract gains one OPTIONAL top-level key:
      "expectation": string   (one sentence, <= 200 chars after strip)
    stating what she expects her record to show on her next tick. Any
    tool call may carry it. Absent or malformed -> ignored, never a
    parse failure (the tool_name/arguments contract is untouched).
  - Runtime holds the most recent (tick_id, text) in memory (maxlen 1,
    same lifetime as the 22.10 carryover) and renders it on the NEXT
    tick, immediately above [Your record]:
      [Your expectation, stated on tick <seq>]
        "<text>"
    Empty -> block omitted (default prompt unchanged until she uses it).
  - The runtime does NOT score, grade, gate, or reward the expectation.
    The comparison is hers to make; gap_assessment is where it lands if
    she makes it. Scoring (calibration, whether gap text references the
    record) is eval-time analysis in scripts, never on the surface.
  - Audit: adapter_audit["expectation"] = text (or absent) on every tick
    so the eval can pair expectation with the record block rendered on
    the following tick. HeartbeatRecord schema is NOT changed.
  - Prompt (additions only, 22.9 voice):
      Output contract gains one line after the JSON shape:
        'Optional key "expectation": one sentence — what you expect
         your record to show on your next tick. It is shown back to
         you next tick beside the actual record.'
      emit_heartbeat's argument list gains an honest description of the
      field that had none: "gap_assessment — where the record differs
      from what you expected, or what you cannot yet see from here."
      No exhortation to use either.
  - Config: PromptConfig.tick_expectation_loop: bool = False. Off ->
    parse ignores the key, no block, prompt line absent — byte-identical
    to 22.10's surface. Live yaml: true.

Part A config: PromptConfig.tick_record_block: bool = False; live yaml
true. Both new flags and the default-mode field follow the 22.6/22.7/
22.8 precedent: defaults reproduce current prompts byte-for-byte, live
config enabling is IN SCOPE for this stage's DoD (22.8's rule against
adding dormant switches), with the flip TIMED per SEQUENCING below.

Register handling: [Your record] and the expectation block render in
both registers. In the exploratory register they sit above the 22.1
recall block; the exploratory rules are untouched. Token cost: record
block <= ~250 tokens, expectation <= ~60, against 32k.

EVALUATION
----------

Sources: logs/traces/*.operational.jsonl (adapter_audit: tool_requested,
arguments incl. recall_history mode, expectation), heartbeats.jsonl,
explorations.jsonl + journal.jsonl, self_model_proposals.jsonl. Compare
the 22.11 window against 08-20 -> go-live (the 22.9/22.10/Part D
baseline).

Primary signals (the "wonder metric" — form AND content):
  - distinct gap_assessment texts per day and distinct next_inquiry
    texts per day, deduplicated by bigram overlap at 0.70 — NOT raw
    non-empty rate (Era 3 shows non-empty rate can recover while content
    stays one sentence).
  - expectation fill rate (ticks carrying the key / ticks), and whether
    gap_assessment on the following tick references the record or the
    expectation. The strongest possible sign: a number from the block
    ("110 times", "0 by you", "16 skipped") appearing in her own words —
    a fact she did not write, landing.
Secondary:
  - recall_history mode distribution (does 'sample' as default change
    what she then does with it; does she ever choose 'earliest'/'around'
    unprompted).
  - first Nova-applied update_self_model since 08-19 (self-model line
    goes from "0 by you" to "1 by you" on her own surface — the block
    makes her own writes visible to her as a count).
  - exploration topic distinctness by bigram overlap, not string
    equality (802 distinct strings / 1,062 is lexical noise).
  - register discipline: actions_blocked stays 0 in both registers.
Null result (must be recorded as a finding, not silence): block
rendered, expectation key never or rarely emitted, gap/next_inquiry
distinctness flat -> "facts about herself that she did not author do
not produce discrepancy for this model on this surface." That is a
substantive negative for the thesis at 14B and should be written as
such — a record that would rather be true than tidy (22.5 language).
It would also be the strongest argument yet that the next lever is
item 3 (event-triggered ticks) or an external text source, not another
tick-surface change.

NON-GOALS / DEFERRED (each a separate operator decision)
--------------------------------------------------------

  - Item 3: event-triggered ticks instead of the 300s clock (a beat
    fires when something arrived: operator message, rotation, rejected
    export, a record line that changed). Larger change to the daemon
    and the budget model; the honest "nothing happened" beat is a
    symptom of the clock, and this stage does not touch the clock.
  - A self-echo gate on emit_heartbeat (reject an observation at >=0.70
    overlap with the last N, reusing the 22.1 machinery). Deliberately
    excluded: it is a negative constraint that manufactures variety
    without a referent; Part A shows her the overlap instead of
    enforcing against it.
  - An external text source on the tick surface (the operator's book
    is the candidate — her philosophical origin, never read by her).
  - Persisting the expectation or the session counters across
    rotations.
  - Any scoring, grading, or reward of expectations on the surface.
  - Raw exploration-journal deep-read (22.10 exclusion stands).
  - NOVA_SOUL.md, the drive, the claim gate, ladder rules, F3 classifier
    (22.4 part B), ladder dedup at the record level.
