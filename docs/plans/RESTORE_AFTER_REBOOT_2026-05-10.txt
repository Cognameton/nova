Architecture state, brief
-------------------------

- Actor (model output): runtime.respond() and runtime.model_idle_tick().
  Both go through chat-template messages path.
- Observer (interpretation): DeterministicObserver in
  src/nova/agent/observer.py. Wired into respond() before/after each retry.
  Output attached to TraceRecord.observer_record. Not authority.
- Governor (deterministic policy): claim gate + validator + retry policy
  + action permission + audit-review + APPROVED_BY_BLOCKLIST. Now also
  consults Observer findings for retry signals (narrator_voice_detected,
  scaffold_echo:<block_name>). Final authority on every decision.
- Operational runner: src/nova/agent/operational_autonomy.py. Step-driven.
  Persists per-session state in <data_dir>/operational_autonomy/.
  Records ticks via JsonlTraceLogger.log_operational_tick.
