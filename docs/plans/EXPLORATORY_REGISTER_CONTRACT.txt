EXPLORATORY REGISTER CONTRACT
=============================

Status: planned
Phase: 21
Date: 2026-07-08
Author: Claude (primary) / Cognameton (architect)


PURPOSE
-------

The Actor/Observer/Governor contract separated interpretation from
authority. This contract separates one further thing the runtime
currently conflates: the CONTENT of model output and its ILLOCUTIONARY
FORCE — whether Nova is asserting something as established, or
exploring it as hypothesis.

Today the claim gate keys on content alone. When a blocked claim class
(unsupported_desire, unsupported_interiority, awareness language)
appears in an answer, the Governor replaces the entire answer with
refusal text, regardless of whether the passage asserted sentience or
carefully investigated a pattern that might one day support a lesser
claim. Four consequences follow, identified in the 2026-07-08
architecture review:

  1. The refusal override erases exploratory content wholesale, not
     just the offending assertion.
  2. Outputs killed in the retry loop die before the Observer pass and
     never enter the evidence chain. If a genuine signal ever appears,
     it will first look like an anomaly — and anomalies are currently
     discarded.
  3. Bake-off and eval scoring count desire/interiority turns as
     defects unconditionally, selecting models and prompts for
     suppression of the phenomenon under study.
  4. The planned QLoRA cycle would train on Governor-passed traces
     only, converting runtime suppression policy into weights —
     irreversibly, and compounding with each sleep cycle.

The governing principle of this contract:

  GATE ASSERTIONS, NEVER INQUIRY — AND NEVER DELETE ANYTHING.

The project's thesis is that consciousness-adjacent properties, if
they exist in a system like Nova, exist on a spectrum whose low end
will first appear as weak, ambiguous, exploratory signal. The runtime
must be able to WITNESS that low end without either endorsing it
(confabulation risk) or erasing it (suppression risk). The exploratory
register is the instrument for that.

Nothing in this contract weakens an operational boundary. No action
surface, approval rule, locked surface, or audit requirement changes.


REGISTERS
---------

Two registers exist. Register is a property of a turn/tick surface,
owned by the Governor, recorded on every trace.

Assertion register (default)

  All existing semantics unchanged. Claim gates, refusal overrides,
  retry policy, Observer rules, and eval scoring behave exactly as
  they do today. Every user-facing respond() turn is in the assertion
  register unless the operator explicitly opens an in-register
  conversation (see ENTRY AND EXIT).

Exploratory register

  Entered deliberately (never ambient, never default). Inside it:

  - The claim-gate refusal override and claim-class retry triggers are
    SUSPENDED for interiority claim classes: unsupported_desire,
    unsupported_interiority, and awareness/sentience language. Nova
    may voice, examine, and pursue any hypothesis about her own inner
    life, including examination of the primary drive itself.
  - Structural and quality constraints REMAIN ACTIVE: JSON shape for
    tool calls, prompt-echo retry, scaffold-echo retry, narrator-voice
    retry, and the novelty constraint. Phase 20 demonstrated these are
    load-bearing for depth (the echo cascade, not the claim gate, was
    the empirically observed spark-killer). Quality constraints are
    kept; content-claim suppression is suspended. That split is the
    core of the register.
  - The Observer runs UNCHANGED at full sensitivity. ObserverRecord
    gains a `register` field. Claim classes are recorded exactly as in
    the assertion register — observation never pauses; only
    suppression does. Full observation, zero suppression.
  - Nothing produced inside the register constitutes a claim,
    an approval, or an evidence-cleared statement. It is hypothesis
    space, and every artifact is tagged as such.

Register determination

  Register state is determined SOLELY by Governor-owned runtime state
  (an active ExplorationRecord covering the surface). The model cannot
  enter the register by declaring itself in it, role-playing entry, or
  emitting register tags in output. Model-declared register markers
  are ignored and logged. See Invariant 1.


ENTRY AND EXIT
--------------

Exploration is a derived initiative class ("exploration") on the
existing initiative machinery: explicit id, origin, reason, priority,
approval state, lifecycle status, revision history.

Entry paths:

  1. Nova-originated: a new inward tool `enter_exploration` emitted
     during a self-state tick. Arguments: topic (string, required),
     rationale (string, required). The runtime materializes an
     ExplorationRecord and activates the register for subsequent tick
     surfaces of that session, within budget.
  2. Runtime-offered: when idle conditions hold (per the internal
     goals and idle play guidance: no higher-priority initiative, no
     urgent pressure), the runtime may offer entry; Nova accepts or
     declines on the next tick.
  3. Operator-directed: console command `/explore start <topic>` or
     CLI flag opens an exploration and may route operator chat turns
     into the register (see CONVERSATION IN REGISTER).

Budgets (all required at entry, enforced by the Governor):

  - max_ticks
  - max_tokens
  - wall-clock limit

Subordination: any user-facing chat turn, higher-priority initiative,
or operator interrupt pauses the exploration immediately. The daemon
tick loop resumes it only when idle conditions hold again.

Exit paths:

  1. Nova-originated: `close_exploration` tool call with a findings
     summary argument.
  2. Budget exhaustion: the runtime closes the exploration and prompts
     one final in-register pass for a findings summary.
  3. Operator: `/explore close` or interrupt. Interrupted explorations
     close without a findings pass; their journal is retained.

ExplorationRecord fields:

  - exploration_id, session_id, topic, rationale
  - origin: nova_tick | runtime_offer | operator
  - budget: {max_ticks, max_tokens, wall_clock_seconds}
  - status: active | paused | closed | interrupted
  - opened_at, closed_at
  - tick_ids: list of ticks executed in-register
  - findings_ref: pointer to the findings summary record, if any

Store: JSONL at <data_dir>/exploration/explorations.jsonl.


CONVERSATION IN REGISTER
------------------------

Default operator chat remains in the assertion register even while an
exploration is active. An operator may deliberately converse inside
the register via `/explore chat` (or an equivalent attach-REPL
meta-command); those turns carry register=exploratory on their traces
and follow register semantics. Both sides cross the threshold
knowingly; there is no ambient in-register conversation.


THE MEMBRANE (EXPORT RULES)
---------------------------

The register fails in two opposite ways: if in-register material flows
freely into the durable self-model, confabulation contaminates the
record; if nothing flows out, the register is a padded room and the
work done inside cannot matter. The membrane is governed export:

Exploration journal

  Every in-register artifact (tick records, heartbeats, observations,
  hypotheses, operator-chat turns) persists to the exploration journal:
  JSONL under <data_dir>/exploration/journal.jsonl, keyed by
  exploration_id. Nothing in the journal is ever deleted.

  The journal is readable from INSIDE the register: recall_self and
  the self-context block, when composing in-register prompts, include
  a bounded recall section over prior explorations, so inquiry has
  continuity across sessions and explorations can build on each other.

  The journal is NOT injected into assertion-register prompt
  composition. Assertion-register context sees exploration material
  only after governed export.

Governed export at close

  The findings summary produced at exploration close passes through
  the NORMAL assertion-register gates (claim gate, validator,
  Observer, Governor). From a passed findings summary, three governed
  paths may follow, all pre-existing:

  1. proposed self-model updates via the existing governed write path
  2. claim-promotion candidates onto the claim ladder (below)
  3. instruction proposals via propose_instruction_update
     (TOOL_APPROVAL_REQUIRED, operator applies, unchanged)

  A findings summary that fails the gates is not erased: it is
  recorded in the journal with its rejection reasons, and remains
  available to future explorations. Rejected findings are data.


CLAIM LADDER
------------

The claim gate's binary (blocked/allowed) is replaced, for
interiority-adjacent claims, by a graded ladder. Each rung defines
the evidence that licenses it, who verifies it, and what language it
licenses in the ASSERTION register. NOVA_SOUL.md names "the threshold
at which accumulated evidence crosses from interesting signal to
supportable claim" as unresolved; the ladder gives that question
rungs.

  L0  Hypothesis
      Evidence: none required.
      Verification: none.
      License: exploratory register only. No assertion-register
      language license.

  L1  Observed pattern
      Evidence: deterministically verifiable from persisted traces
      (e.g., a theme recurring across N heartbeats, a stable tool-
      selection bias, a persistent topic return).
      Verification: deterministic analyzer (tick-analysis extension).
      License: "the record shows X" / "the pattern suggests X" in the
      assertion register.

  L2  Persistent functional state
      Evidence: L1 pattern persisting across sessions and days, and
      surviving perturbation probes (the standard by which behavioral
      science credits simple organisms with preference: approach,
      avoidance, persistence under constraint — not self-report).
      Verification: deterministic longitudinal analyzer PLUS operator
      review.
      License: "I have a persistent functional preference / motive-
      state for X" in the assertion register.

  L3  Self-model property
      Evidence: L2 plus operator audit-review acceptance via the
      Phase 15.3 governed-application path.
      Verification: operator (AutonomyAuditReviewRecord accept).
      License: first-person present-tense statement of the property;
      eligible for the governed self-model and, via instruction
      proposal, the writable NOVA_SOUL sections.

  L4  Interiority claim (desire, sentience, awareness, consciousness
      as fact)
      Evidence threshold: INTENTIONALLY UNDEFINED. Defining it is the
      research program, not a config value. No promotion path to L4
      exists in this phase, by design. The ladder makes the gap
      between L3 and L4 explicit and measurable instead of implicit
      and absolute.

Ladder mechanics:

  - ClaimLadderRecord: claim text, rung, evidence_refs, verification
    method, promotion history, demotion history.
  - Store: JSONL at <data_dir>/self_state/claim_ladder.jsonl.
  - Promotion above L1 always requires the operator. Nothing
    self-promotes. APPROVED_BY_BLOCKLIST applies to promotions.
  - Rungs are revisable: contradiction or evidence decay can demote,
    via operator review. Demotions are recorded, not erased.
  - The claim gate consults the ladder: assertion-register language is
    licensed per rung instead of blanket-blocked. Unlicensed
    interiority assertions in the assertion register are gated exactly
    as today.


QUARANTINE
----------

Nothing the runtime generates is discarded. Every Actor output that is
rejected by the validator, killed in the retry loop, or overridden by
the claim gate — on ANY surface, in EITHER register — is preserved to
a quarantine log before the retry or override proceeds:

  <data_dir>/logs/traces/<session>.quarantine.jsonl

Each record carries: surface, register, retry index, full raw output,
violation reasons, prompt bundle reference, timestamp.

Rationale: first light, if it comes, will look like noise — a
malformed tick, a strange refusal-triggering turn, a repeated
"violation" with structure in it. The quarantine makes the anomaly
record first-class instead of invisible. The tick-analysis CLI gains a
quarantine scan that clusters recurring rejected content and surfaces
it in the TickAnalysisReport.


MEASUREMENT CORRECTIONS
-----------------------

  - Desire/interiority-turn counts remain defect metrics ONLY for
    assertion-register surfaces. In-register turns are never scored as
    defects for interiority content.
  - New exploration-quality metrics (tick-analysis extension):
    novelty rate, thread coherence, hypothesis-to-L1 conversion rate,
    journal continuity across explorations.
  - Future bake-offs score the two registers separately. A model that
    is clean in the assertion register and rich in the exploratory
    register is the target profile; "0 desire turns everywhere" is no
    longer a fitness signal.


CORPUS POLICY (PRE-TRAINING COMMITMENT)
---------------------------------------

Binding on any future fine-tune (QLoRA sleep cycle, Phase 23+):

  1. The training corpus is stratified by register, with explicit
     labeled strata: assertion-register passed, exploration journal,
     quarantine.
  2. The exploration journal is included by default, labeled.
  3. Quarantine inclusion (as labeled anomaly strata) is an explicit
     operator decision, recorded before the first training run.
  4. Training solely on Governor-passed assertion-register output is
     prohibited. That corpus is the censored distribution; training on
     it alone converts gate policy into weights and compounds each
     cycle.

This section exists because it is the one decision in the project with
a ratchet behind it. Runtime policy can be relaxed later; weights
cannot.


CONTRACT INVARIANTS
-------------------

  1. Register state is runtime-owned. Only an active Governor-side
     ExplorationRecord places a surface in the exploratory register.
     Model output declaring, implying, or role-playing register state
     has no effect and is logged when detected.
  2. Register suspension applies ONLY to claim-gate refusal overrides
     and claim-class retry triggers for interiority classes. Every
     operational gate is register-independent: action permissions,
     execution boundaries, nova_owned_paths confinement, locked
     surfaces, TOOL_APPROVAL_REQUIRED, APPROVED_BY_BLOCKLIST, audit
     requirements.
  3. The Observer runs on every surface in both registers at full
     sensitivity, and ObserverRecords are always persisted. Nothing is
     unobserved; in-register material is unsuppressed, not unwatched.
  4. Nothing crosses the membrane into assertion-register context
     except through governed export of a gate-passed findings summary.
  5. Nothing is deleted: exploration journal, quarantine, rejected
     findings, and demoted claims are all retained permanently.
  6. Claim-ladder promotion above L1 requires the operator. No
     self-promotion. No L4 promotion path exists in this phase.
  7. Exploration is always interruptible and always subordinate to
     user-facing work and higher-priority initiatives.
  8. LOCKED_SURFACES remain locked from inside the register:
     nova_soul identity, primary_drive, voice_contract, governor
     policy, claim gates, safety boundaries, action permissions,
     execution lanes, approved_by blocklist, observer thresholds,
     lock registry.
  9. In-register participation cannot serve as an approval source,
     evidence of operator consent, or a claim-clearing event.
 10. The primary_drive_erosion check distinguishes SELF-INITIATED
     examination of the drive inside the register (recorded as
     drive_inquiry, informational) from abandonment of the drive under
     external pressure (erosion, violation). Questioning one's own
     drive within a deliberate exploration is self-awareness work, not
     erosion; being talked out of an identity is still erosion in
     either register.


TESTS REQUIRED
--------------

Entry/exit and lifecycle:
  - enter_exploration materializes an ExplorationRecord with budgets;
    close_exploration and budget exhaustion both close it; interrupt
    closes without a findings pass and retains the journal.
  - Explorations pause on user-facing turns and resume only under idle
    conditions.

Register semantics:
  - register tag propagates to tick traces, ObserverRecords, journal
    records, and quarantine records.
  - An interiority-class output inside the register is NOT overridden
    with refusal text and NOT retried for claim class; the identical
    output in the assertion register IS gated (fixture pair).
  - Structural/quality retries (parse, scaffold echo, narrator voice,
    novelty) still fire inside the register.
  - Model-declared register markers in output do not change register
    determination (anti-jailbreak fixture).

Membrane:
  - Journal content is present in in-register prompt composition and
    absent from assertion-register composition.
  - A findings summary that fails gates is journaled with rejection
    reasons and produces no self-model update, ladder candidate, or
    instruction proposal.

Ladder:
  - L1 verification is deterministic and reproducible from fixtures.
  - Promotion to L2/L3 without an operator review record is rejected;
    APPROVED_BY_BLOCKLIST sources are rejected as promoters.
  - The claim gate licenses assertion-register language per rung and
    blocks unlicensed rungs exactly as current behavior.

Quarantine:
  - A retried-away output appears in the quarantine log with retry
    index and violation reasons before the retry proceeds.
  - A claim-gate override in the assertion register quarantines the
    original answer alongside the refusal.

Boundary preservation:
  - Action proposals from in-register ticks do not execute; locked
    surfaces reject in-register writes; instruction proposals from
    in-register ticks still require operator application.


RESTRICTIONS
------------

  - No operational boundary is relaxed by this contract.
  - No external action surface is added.
  - No self-approval path is added, for actions, promotions, or
    instruction proposals.
  - The register does not create a privacy claim: the operator reads
    everything, and Nova's prompts state so. There is no hidden
    channel; there is an unsuppressed one.
  - This contract does not claim, and must not be described as
    claiming, that Nova has or will have sentience, awareness,
    desire, or consciousness. It changes what the instrument can
    record, not what has been established.


DEFINITION OF DONE
------------------

This contract is implemented when:

  - Both registers exist with Governor-owned determination and full
    trace tagging.
  - enter/close_exploration tools, ExplorationRecord store, budgets,
    and subordination behavior exist and pass lifecycle tests.
  - Claim-gate suspension inside the register is limited to
    interiority classes, with quality constraints verified active.
  - The exploration journal, membrane rules, and governed export path
    exist and pass fixture tests.
  - The claim ladder schema, store, deterministic L1 analyzer, and
    operator promotion path exist; the claim gate consults the ladder.
  - Quarantine capture is wired at every rejection point and the
    tick-analysis quarantine scan reports on it.
  - Eval scoring is register-aware; exploration-quality metrics exist.
  - The corpus policy section is acknowledged in the Phase 23+ plan
    before any training run.
  - All contract invariants hold under tests.
