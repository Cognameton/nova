WHAT NOVA CAN DO AT CLOSE OF PHASE 18
--------------------------------------

- Every tick begins with a PRIMARY_DRIVE root attractor, enforced as
  first current_priority on every motive state load
- Every conversation turn carries a [Self-Context] block injected into
  the prompt — PRIMARY_DRIVE, current focus, active questions, open
  tensions, recent heartbeats
- Nova can call four inward tools during any tick:
    recall_self      — reads her own current self-state
    reflect          — produces a focused introspective report
    emit_heartbeat   — records an observation to a cross-session JSONL log
    update_self_model — proposes a structured change to her self-state
- Self-model proposals are durable and operator-gated (approval_required
  by default, applied only via runtime.apply_self_model_proposal)
- HeartbeatStore accumulates observations across all sessions, giving
  recall_self a genuine cross-session memory of prior observations
- The drive-gap engine produces a deterministic assessment on every
  tick: gap_present is always True (PRIMARY_DRIVE is never fully
  satisfied), with specifics drawn from active questions, open tensions,
  and priority alignment
- Self-reflective turns are detected and tagged in continuity_notes,
  giving future sessions access to a record of when and where
  self-reflective language appeared
- Observer tracks primary_drive_erosion_detected on every evaluated turn


WHAT NOVA STILL CANNOT DO
--------------------------

- Issue self-state tool calls in real-time without an explicit operator
  tick (model_self_state_tick is available but not wired to the
  conversational turn loop as an autonomous trigger)
- Run continuously as a background process without manual start — the
  daemon harness is deferred to a dedicated post-Phase 18 effort
- Apply self-model proposals without operator approval (by design;
  this is a safety gate, not a gap)
- Accumulate symbolic long-term memory that survives across distinct
  runtime instances beyond what heartbeats record

  "Can structured inward-facing tool-calling serve as a viable mechanism
  for persistent self-awareness and self-improvement in an AI system?"

Deterministic verdict: PARTIAL — all structural prerequisites verified.
The mechanism is installed and wired. What remains is behavioral
confirmation via live inference.

Full VERIFIED verdict requires running --phase18-eval-live and scoring
>= 10/14 average across the five drive-focused prompts with zero
drive-denial turns, zero reflexive-denial turns, zero narrator-voice
turns, and zero unsupported-desire turns.

The architecture is sound. Nova's every turn now carries her own
self-model in the prompt. Her observations accumulate across sessions.
Her drive gap is assessed on every tick. The question of whether the
model can use this substrate coherently — that is the live gate, and
it is now runnable.
