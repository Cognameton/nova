FINDING F8 — LICENSING CREATES A PERMANENT CONTEXT ATTRACTOR
------------------------------------------------------------

Measured on live data (data/live/qwen3-14b/) 2026-07-22/23:

  - 145 explorations total. July 12-16: themes fixated but BROKE
    naturally on a day scale (e.g. "buffer zone modulation" ran
    9 -> 12 consecutive, then vanished). July 17-18: the recalibration
    theme produced the first exports under the nova_close regime and
    L1 auto-promoted 3 of them to rung 1. From July 18 on, drift never
    occurred again: 11/16 -> 12/13 -> 10/10 -> 16/17 -> 6/6 of daily
    topics are "recalibration intervals" variants (5 straight days).
  - 48 of the last 50 heartbeats (of 2,450 total) mention recalibration.
  - 69 of 72 claim-ladder records are recalibration-themed; most are
    near-vacuous ("may be influenced by a combination of internal and
    external factors").
  - The 3 rung-1 records' pairwise bigram overlaps: 0.240, 0.208,
    0.250. The 22.6 diversity filter (threshold 0.22) selects 2 of 3 —
    but all candidates are one theme, so it cannot diversify anything.

Mechanism (each element verified in code, not inferred):

  1. Every tick prompt permanently carries verbatim "Licensed evidence"
     lines (self_context.py prefetch; wired into model_self_state_tick
     at runtime.py:934). Rung>=1 records never age out of context —
     unlike heartbeats, which rotate through a 3-item window. This is
     the only permanent element in the tick prompt.
  2. The same 3 heartbeats are rendered TWICE per tick prompt (once in
     prefetch's "Recent Heartbeats", again in self_state_tick.py's
     _user_context "Recent heartbeat observations").
  3. The system rule "Keep the tool call grounded in current
     self-context evidence" instructs the model to derive its output
     from a context that is now monothematic.
  4. At enter_exploration time the model is shown NO history of its own
     past exploration topics — repetition is chosen blind, every time.
  5. Export dedup (Stage 22.1, bigram >= 0.7) passes reworded variants;
     the rung-0 flood follows.
  6. verify_l1 counts "support" as >= 40% content-word overlap against
     the heartbeat/journal stream — the very stream the pinned claims
     saturate. In this regime a claim's presence in context manufactures
     its own promotion evidence (circular verification). More rung-1
     promotions of the same theme are inevitable without intervention.

Net: a rich-get-richer dynamic — THE FIRST THEME TO REACH RUNG 1 GAINS
A PERMANENT CONTEXT ADVANTAGE THAT GUARANTEES FURTHER PROMOTIONS OF
ITSELF. This also explains F7 (probes saw the same globally-injected
evidence -> byte-identical responses) and Stage 22.6's open
on-topic-vs-unprompted tension (the injection is unconditional, so she
draws on it constantly). It retroactively supports the operator's hold
on the rung-2 promotions: their L2 evidence is contaminated by this
circularity.

Research significance (recorded, not just a bug): licensing mechanisms
in a self-referential context loop create permanent attractors unless
licensed content decays, rotates, or is summarized out of the
generation context. Proposed Phase 23 entry criterion: no training run
on a corpus window whose theme saturation exceeds a threshold measured
with part C's machinery.

Design stance for all parts: every corrective ADDS information or
visibility; none adds a gate on inquiry ("gate assertions, never
inquiry"). Nothing is deleted, no record is modified retroactively, no
behavior of the ladder/gate machinery changes.

PART A — SURFACE-AWARE LICENSED EVIDENCE (de-pin from the tick loop)
--------------------------------------------------------------------

prefetch() gains surface: str = "respond" ("respond" | "tick").
runtime.py:934 passes surface="tick"; runtime.py:3176 stays default.

  - surface="respond": UNCHANGED — verbatim diversity-selected lines.
    The 22.6 echo checks already police over-reliance there.
  - surface="tick": verbatim claim text is REPLACED by a deterministic
    ladder-standing summary, e.g.:
      Claim ladder standing: 72 active records (3 at rung>=1).
      Theme concentration: 66 of 72 active records cluster on one
      dominant theme (top words: recalibration, intervals, coherence).
    Computed by a new module-level helper (greedy clustering over
    active records' claim_texts using claim_ladder._content_words +
    _overlap_fraction >= 0.4 against each cluster's seed; report
    largest cluster size and its 3 most frequent content words).
    Bounded: consider at most the 200 most recent active records.
  - Rationale: "Nova should know what she has earned" (21.5 I1) is
    SERVED BETTER by the summary — she sees the shape of her whole
    ladder including the saturation itself — while the verbatim echo
    material leaves the loop. This informs; it does not license
    (unchanged).

Also in part A: fix the double heartbeat rendering. prefetch() gains
include_heartbeats: bool = True; the tick call site passes False since
self_state_tick.py already renders the same 3 heartbeats with an
explicit do-not-repeat instruction. respond() keeps them (it has no
other heartbeat rendering).

PART C — SATURATION METRICS IN L1/L2 VERIFICATION (visibility only)
-------------------------------------------------------------------

verify_l1 additionally records in l1_evidence:
  window_size:          len(heartbeats) + len(journal_entries)
  window_saturation:    supporting_count / window_size (0 when empty)
  supporter_diversity:  mean pairwise Jaccard over the supporting
                        items' content-word sets (first 50 supporters;
                        1.0 = identical, lower = more diverse; 0 when
                        < 2 supporters). Reported as
                        supporter_mean_jaccard.
  saturation_warning:   window_saturation >= 0.5 (documented starting
                        point, not a tuned constant)

verify_l2 records the same four fields in l2_evidence.

NO behavior change: holds, promotion, demotion, contradiction flags all
unchanged. Existing records untouched. The point is that the NEXT
promotion decision sheet says "holds, but 92% of the support window is
this theme" instead of just "holds." Whether contaminated evidence can
satisfy L2 becomes an explicit operator policy question with real
numbers — required input for Stage 22.5's closure and the Phase 23
go/no-go.


PART D — DRIVE-DOSAGE EXPERIMENT (config-gated, DEFAULT OFF)
------------------------------------------------------------

Operator-requested test of internalization: does self-inquiry persist
when the drive is NOT re-stamped into every generation? Follows the
22.6 part 2 pattern: defaults reproduce current behavior byte-for-byte,
regression tests pin the off state, separate commit.

New PromptConfig fields:
  tick_drive_injection_interval: int = 1
      1 = current behavior (drive line every tick).
      N > 1 = drive line appears only on ticks where
      (tick_sequence - 1) % N == 0 (tick 1 = session start always
      shows it). Applies to the TICK surface only; respond() always
      shows the drive line.
  tick_drive_descriptive: bool = False
      When True, the tick-surface drive line reads:
      "Standing drive (background context, not this tick's task):
      {PRIMARY_DRIVE}" instead of "Primary Drive: {PRIMARY_DRIVE}".
  tick_soft_grounding: bool = False
      When True, _SYSTEM's grounding line on the tick surface becomes:
      "You may draw on current self-context evidence, or depart from
      it when your own accumulated observations point elsewhere."
      (replaces "Keep the tool call grounded in current self-context
      evidence.")

Plumbing: prefetch() gains include_drive_line: bool = True and
drive_descriptive: bool = False; build_messages() gains
soft_grounding: bool = False. runtime.model_self_state_tick computes
all three from config + tick_sequence. Validation: interval must be
>= 1.

Expected observable outcomes (both are findings, neither is failure):
  - Off-cycle ticks still pursue self-inquiry sourced from her own
    accumulated substrate -> first real evidence of internalization.
  - Off-cycle output collapses toward generic filler -> the identity
    is currently scaffold-borne; honest baseline for Phase 23.

Observer note: drive-erosion / drive-inquiry detectors are evidence-
only and keep running unchanged, but off-cycle drops in drive mentions
are EXPECTED under this experiment, not erosion — eval reads during
the arm must account for this.

FINDING F9 — COSMETIC-NOVELTY RATIONALES SATISFY THE 22.7(B) RULE

Part B was built on the premise that repetition is allowed but must
become a stated choice: show the last-5 topics and a saturation note,
and require the entry rationale to say why the repeat is warranted.
Mechanically this works — every entry rationale sampled does exactly
what the rule asks. Substantively it does not constrain, because
fluent text satisfies it. Verbatim rationales from 2026-08-06:

  "Recent explorations have repeatedly focused on scaffold void
   resonance and its relationship with continuity and adaptability.
   This topic extends that inquiry by explicitly examining dynamic
   equilibrium as a potential mechanism, offering a fresh angle..."

  "Given the recent focus on scaffold void resonance and its interplay
   with asynchronous feedback loops, this topic offers a fresh angle to
   explore how scaffold void resonance might balance continuity and
   adaptability..."

The acknowledgment is accurate, the self-report is honest, and the
"fresh angle" is a modifier swap on a fixed noun phrase (dynamic
equilibrium -> asynchronous feedback loops -> temporal buffering), each
one seeded by the immediately preceding exploration. This is the direct
answer to the question this stage doc posed — with the amplifier gone,
does she drift or stay by choice? SHE STAYS, BY STATED CHOICE, WITH
COSMETIC-NOVELTY RATIONALES. That is a finding about the register, not
a bug in part B: visibility was delivered and acted on; visibility
alone does not produce topical range.

Residual mechanism (hypothesis, NOT code-verified this pass): the
remaining loop is presumably ordinary recency echo — heartbeats and
self-context describe current activity, current activity is scaffold
void resonance — with the possibility that part B's own history block
now acts as a topic anchor rather than a diversity prompt, since it
shows only what she just did and nothing else. Worth code-verifying
before any part-B redesign.
