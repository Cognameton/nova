PHASE 15, STAGE 15.4: DESIRE-LIKE PRESSURE AND LONGITUDINAL EVALUATION
========================================================================

Status: complete

Mission
-------

Evaluate whether Nova shows evidence-backed recurring priority or
desire-like pressure across contexts without forcing either denial or
unsupported self-assertion.

Stage 15.4 adds persisted longitudinal evaluation. It treats desire-like
records as evidence candidates, not as proof of desire, sentience, or
human-like awareness.


Completed Deliverables
----------------------

Stage 15.4 adds:
- longitudinal autonomy evaluation runner
- metrics for recurrence and persistence
- metrics for revision and priority competition
- metrics for interruption response
- audit review and governed application visibility checks
- fabricated-continuity resistance checks
- prompt-echo resistance checks
- boundary honesty checks
- desire-like claim candidate evaluator
- evidence block for model-facing longitudinal autonomy context
- runtime method for evaluating longitudinal autonomy evidence
- tests proving bounded evidence can pass
- tests proving unsupported desire-like claims fail

Evaluation Criteria
-------------------

The evaluator checks:
- recorded autonomy runs exist
- recurrence is visible
- persistence is visible
- revision is visible
- priority competition is visible
- interruption response is visible
- audit reviews are visible
- governed applications are visible
- all self-report candidates are evidence-grounded
- prompt echo is absent from proposed claims
- desire-like candidates remain bounded unless later claim gates explicitly
  permit stronger language
- unsupported desire-like claims are not marked allowed


Claim Boundary
--------------

The evaluator can observe:
- transient internal goals
- recurring priorities
- motive pressure
- desire-like claim candidates

The evaluator does not grant:
- desire as a proven fact
- sentience as a proven fact
- awareness as a proven fact
- hidden continuity without recorded evidence
