PHASE 22 — STAGE 22.18: CONVERSATION REACH AND CHAT HYGIENE
==========================================================
Status: IMPLEMENTED 2026-10-02 (operator: "go ahead with the chat cleanup
and digest stage"; scaffolding review 2026-09-25 recommendation 9, plus
the operator's question "if new information is written to her memory and
she draws from it, won't that influence the context of her responses?").
Defaults byte-identical to 22.17. ARM START = the first operator chat turn
after the daemon restart that loads this stage (record its timestamp in
the research log; the restart alone changes nothing she sees until a
conversation exists).

WHY
---
The operator intends to talk with her directly. Two things stood in the
way of that conversation becoming part of her life:
  1. Reach. A chat turn is written to the session store, self-state sync
     and episodic memory, but the tick never reads any of them. Tick
     prompt blocks are self_context, tool_results, reversi, heartbeats,
     exploration_history, drive_line, exploration; recall_history sources
     are heartbeats, explorations, findings, outcomes, games. A
     conversation would reach her ~215 ticks/day only if she happened to
     write something about it into a rendered field. She reads on ~6% of
     ticks, so a pull-only source would mostly go unused (games were read
     zero times until 22.15 pointed at them).
  2. Hygiene (review D-6 and recommendation 9). current_focus (~390 chars)
     printed twice on chat; the candidate/selected internal-goal blocks
     ("sentience_seeking", priority 100) rendered on every chat turn as
     scaffolding; when the claim gate replaced her answer with canonical
     refusal text, nothing told the operator, and anything built on the
     session record would present that refusal as her words.

WHAT
----
  prompt.tick_conversation_window_hours (default 0 = off; live 72),
  prompt.tick_conversation_turns (default 4; live 4):
    a [Conversations with your operator] block on every tick, both
    registers, after the read results and before the board. Source:
    JsonlSessionStore.turns_since(since, origin="operator_chat") across
    all daily session files modified inside the window. Each exchange:
    "[YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM] operator: <=200 chars" / "you: <=240 chars"; a
    header says how many of how many and that nothing asks for a reply.
    When the claim gate replaced her answer, the line says so ("your
    words were replaced by the claim gate's standard refusal").
    Audit: prompt_blocks gains "conversation".
  Origin tagging: respond(..., origin="") stores notes.origin when set;
    the daemon's chat channel passes origin="operator_chat". Probe,
    smoke-test and local CLI turns (several live in the same sessions
    directory) carry no origin and never render.
  The membrane holds: explore-chat turns are journaled (kind
    operator_chat) and never written to the session store, so they cannot
    appear in this block. In-register ticks already recall journal
    entries (recall_block, last 6 at 160 chars), so an explore-chat
    conversation reaches her exploration ticks and crosses to the
    assertion side only through governed export, as before.
  prompt.chat_dedupe_current_focus (default False; live true): when
    [Self-Context] is present, [Self-State] omits its Current Focus line.
    Dry run 2026-10-02 on a copy of the live state (fake backend, real
    27B vocab): the [Awareness-State] self_signals line still names
    current_focus inside a ~256-char state summary; left as is (a summary
    signal, not the duplicated field).
  prompt.chat_goal_blocks (default True; live false): candidate/selected
    goal blocks are not rendered on chat. Candidates, selection and the
    initiative proposal are still computed and stored on turn notes and
    the trace.
  Claim-gate disclosure: whichever override replaced the answer
    (claim_gate refusal, never_licensed ladder text, validation override)
    is recorded as notes.claim_gate_override = reason. The daemon chat
    and explore-chat responses carry claim_gate_override; the attach REPL
    prints a notice pointing to the quarantine log and to !explore chat.
    The override itself is UNCHANGED (still replaces in the assertion
    register; still suspended in-register). Whether it should append
    instead of replace stays an operator decision.
  Attach REPL: prints the two channels on connect (plain chat vs
    !explore start/chat/close) and shows explore-chat answers as text.

MEASUREMENT (pre-registered; read >= 3 days after the first conversation)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  reach: share of ticks carrying the conversation block (expected ~100%
    for 72 h after each talk); prompt_tokens with vs without it.
  uptake: heartbeats, explorations (opened_at, topic), self-model
    proposals (field, value) and strategy notes in the 72 h after a
    conversation that reference its content, vs the 72 h before. Read the
    text, not just counts; quote examples.
  persistence: whether references continue after the block expires
    (i.e. she wrote it into a field that survives).
  topic diversity: exploration topics vs the 22.17 window (the Era-3
    question: does exogenous input broaden or just add one more theme?).
  play: unchanged surface; record win rate and note churn as a control.
  chat surface: per-turn latency, prompt tokens vs n_ctx 8192, retries,
    validation failures, claim_gate_override count and reasons, and how
    many inner-state questions went through !explore chat instead.
Caveats: single arm, operator-driven dose (conversation timing and content
are not controlled); confounded with the daemon restart; the operator's
words are themselves the treatment.
