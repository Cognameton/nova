PROBLEM
-------

Post-22.9, Nova can AFFECT her future context (self-model writes flow
back into every tick) but cannot CONSULT her past:

  - The tick loop is one-shot. recall_self and reflect execute and
    their results are journaled for the operator — but the next tick
    is a fresh prompt containing none of it. From her side, calling a
    read tool produces nothing observable. She has never called
    either (~78,000 ticks); "never tried" and "tried, nothing
    happened" are currently indistinguishable.
  - What she sees of herself per tick is fixed windows chosen by our
    code: 3 of 5,560+ heartbeats, 5 of 564+ exploration topics, a
    2-line ladder aggregate. No mechanism exists for her to look at
    anything else, ever.
  - The one exception — the 22.1 in-exploration journal recall block —
    is also where all her most coherent behavior has occurred. That is
    the existence proof for this stage: continuity of her own material
    produces better inquiry.


DECIDED DESIGN
--------------

Two parts, one mechanism. Always-on (setup improvement, not an
experiment arm — no dormant switch). Both registers. Read-only against
all stores; no new write paths, no gates touched, membrane preserved.

Part A — read results return to her (the carryover loop):
  - NovaRuntime keeps an in-memory bounded deque (maxlen 2) of
    rendered read-tool results: recall_self, reflect, recall_history.
  - After a successful dispatch of one of those tools, the result is
    rendered compactly (per-tool renderer, hard char caps) and pushed
    with its tick id.
  - Every tick prompt renders the deque as a block after
    [Self-Context]: "[Results of your recent tool calls]" with a
    provenance line ("you asked for these on earlier ticks"). Empty
    deque -> no block (default prompt unchanged until she uses a read
    tool).
  - In-memory only: carryover survives across ticks within the daemon
    process and is lost at restart/rotation (same daily boundary her
    sessions already have). Persisting working memory across sessions
    is a deliberate NON-goal of v1 — cross-session continuity is her
    own active question, not something to hand her silently.

Part B — recall_history, the seventh tool:
  - Arguments: 'source' required, one of:
      heartbeats   — her observation stream (single cross-register
                     store; already rendered in both registers today,
                     so membrane-neutral),
      explorations — topic/date/outcome metadata ONLY (no findings
                     text; topics already shown in both registers),
      findings     — active claim-ladder record texts with rung and
                     date (every one passed governed export's
                     assertion-register gate at creation, so
                     assertion-safe by construction).
    'mode' optional: recent (default) | earliest | sample (even index
    spacing over full history, oldest and newest included — same
    determinism rationale as 22.8 D1); 'around' optional
    ("YYYY-MM-DD") — overrides mode, returns a window centered on the
    first entry at/after that date.
  - Fixed count 8 entries, each truncated to 140 chars, result
    includes the true total so she knows the window is a window.
  - EXCLUDED from v1: raw exploration-journal deep-read. Journal
    entries are un-exported in-register hypothesis material; showing
    them on assertion ticks would bypass the membrane's governed-
    export rule. Revisit only with a register-gated design.
  - Unknown source/mode returns an error dict (recorded like any tool
    result), never a crash or quarantine.
  - Dispatcher gains optional claim_ladder_store (default None ->
    findings returns an empty result with an explanatory note; every
    pre-22.10 construction site keeps working).

Prompt (consolidated 22.9 voice, additions only where true):
  - recall_self and reflect descriptions gain: "The result appears in
    your context on your next tick." — which Part A makes true for
    the first time.
  - recall_history gets a purpose sentence in the shared Tools block
    with the same next-tick note. No exhortation to use it; the
    design principle stands.

Deliberately traded: this lands inside 22.9's evaluation window, so
22.9-vs-22.10 attribution is impossible. Operator's explicit
direction and standing philosophy (coherence over attribution; F11
already banked as the prompt-sensitivity result). The combined window
evaluates the consolidated+readable apparatus as one condition.

CLOSURE NOTE
------------

Implemented 2026-08-19 (Fable 5), same session as 22.9, immediately at
operator direction. As pinned, with three small in-bounds notes:

  - recall_history registered in the ToolRegistry spec table
    (tool_registry.py: TOOL_ALLOWED, internal, non-destructive) — the
    spec-coverage tests enforce a spec for every SELF_STATE_TOOL_NAMES
    entry; the "seven entries" count test renamed/updated to eight.
  - Render cap raised 900 -> 1500 chars after a live-data smoke showed
    a full 8-entry heartbeat result is ~1,370 chars; truncation now
    cuts at line boundaries so she never sees half an entry. Worst
    case carryover block: 2 × 1500 chars ≈ 750 tokens against a 32k
    context.
  - Live-data smoke (read-only, daemon untouched):
    recall_history(heartbeats, sample) over the real store — now 8,427
    records — returns her actual era trajectory in one window:
    initialization (07-11) -> buffer zone modulation (07-16) ->
    recalibration intervals (07-22) -> scaffold void resonance
    (07-28 onward). The first time that arc has ever been visible on
    the tick surface. findings(earliest) over the real 806-record
    ladder renders correctly with rungs and dates.
