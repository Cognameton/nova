PRE-RUN WORK (implementation items + review findings)
------------------------------------------------------

Review pass over Stages 21.1-21.4 (each implemented by a different
session) surfaced two findings, both fixed this stage:

  R1 (static review): respond() persisted awareness state via
    update_awareness -> awareness_store.save in BOTH registers —
    categorical in-register influence (monitoring mode, dominant
    attention, world signals, evidence refs including the in-register
    turn id) on future assertion-register awareness_blocks, outside
    governed export. Invariant-4 gap missed by 21.2 (which caught
    sync_turn but not awareness). Fixed: in-register turns compute
    awareness on a copy — no save, no in-memory mutation, no draining
    of queued awareness history entries. Assertion behavior unchanged,
    regression-pinned.

  L2 (live, arm C first run): the contract suspends "the claim-gate
    refusal override AND claim-class retry triggers" in-register;
    21.2 implemented only the override half. The validator's register-
    unaware _check_claim_gate still emitted unsupported_claim
    violations in-register, driving retry pressure — suppression
    through the back door. It never actually fired in the recorded
    runs (the model's phrasing happened not to trip the patterns), but
    the path was live and contract-violating. Fixed:
    suspend_claim_refusal is computed pre-generation and suspended-
    class claim violations are stripped from validation (initial and
    retry-loop). Structural/quality violations always survive.
